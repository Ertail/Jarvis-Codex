# Evidence ladder

Prefer higher evidence for claims that control the recommendation.

1. Executed behavior, reproducible data, or authoritative primary record.
2. Official specification, documentation, source code, or first-party statement.
3. Independent high-quality research with transparent method.
4. Multiple consistent secondary sources.
5. Expert interpretation with disclosed assumptions.
6. Model memory or unverified community claim.

When sources conflict, compare version, date, scope, definitions, method, and
incentives. Preserve the disagreement if it cannot be resolved.

Confidence labels:

- **High**: direct evidence and no material contradiction.
- **Medium**: good evidence with limited gaps or inference.
- **Low**: indirect, stale, incomplete, or conflicting evidence.
